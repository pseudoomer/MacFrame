Controlled via MacFrame.app

Real-time frame interpolation, frame rate caps, scene presets, and artifact guards are managed directly by MacFrame.app (or its menu bar dashboard) with zero-latency shared-memory sync.

Open Plugins → MacFrame → Open MacFrame Control Center... to adjust settings while playing.
